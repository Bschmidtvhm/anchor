SSBCI Documentation

What a defensible SSBCI determination file contains

The CY2027 Final Rule changed what a plan has to prove about SSBCI. Published objective criteria. No member self-attestation. Retained evidence for every qualified member, and for every member screened out.

Why this changed

The bar moved. The documentation underneath it is still fragmented.

For plan year 2026, SSBCI carries the book that VBID used to. Every qualifying member now holds an eligibility determination that has to be made on objective criteria, documented, and produced to CMS on request. The benefit design moved quickly. Most plans still assemble the record across a vendor portal, an email thread, a care manager's notes, and a spreadsheet. That record exists. It is not yet a file.

The test

Could you produce this, for any enrollee, on any date, in a week?

At audit, a record spread across four systems is a finding, not a file. Three parts of the record are the ones most plans are missing.

01

The enrollees you found ineligible

Most plans keep the approvals and lose the denials. The denial record is what shows the criteria were applied consistently.

42 CFR 422.102(f)(4)
02

What you published, and when

The obligation is to the objective criteria as published at the time. Dated evidence of what was posted, not what is posted today.

42 CFR 422.102(f)(4)
03

Coverage-year stability and the retention clock

Criteria held without modification for the full coverage year, inside a ten-year retention obligation subject to HHS and Comptroller General inspection.

42 CFR 422.102(f)(4), 422.504(d) and (e)
